How it works

Four small steps, then a report.

  1. Answer 13 questions. Age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG summary, and a few yes/no symptoms.
  2. Dr. Cardio scores them. Standardized features go into a linear Perceptron; the raw score is shown.
  3. Azure AI explains it. Your answers and the score are sent to Azure gpt-5-mini, which writes a plain-language summary.
  4. You get a report. A printable page with the prediction, confidence, AI explanation, and every answer you gave.

About the project

What’s inside, what it can do, and what it can’t.

The data

303 patients, 13 features. Numeric (age, blood pressure, cholesterol, max heart rate, ST depression) and categorical (chest pain type, resting ECG, slope, thal).

The model

A linear Perceptron trained on standardized features. We expose weights, bias, training and test metrics at /api/model no black box.

The AI layer

Azure AI Foundry (deployment gpt-5-mini) reads your answers and the Perceptron’s score, then writes a short, careful explanation. It never changes the prediction itself.

What it can’t do

It can’t diagnose you. It can’t read ECGs or images. It only sees the 13 numbers you type. If you feel unwell, contact a clinician , do not wait for a demo.

Privacy

Your answers are used to compute the score locally, then sent to Azure only for the explanation. They live in the server session for the duration of your visit only.

Why Perceptron

It is the simplest trainable classifier , one weight vector and a bias. That makes every prediction auditable, which matters when demoing health tooling.
